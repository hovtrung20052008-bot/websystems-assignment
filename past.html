My First Impression about Technology

1. A visit to my dad's programming lab

When I was younger, technology seemed complicated and a little intimidating. My dad helped change that when he took me to his programming lab. He showed me projects involving robots and other fascinating technology.Seeing those projects made me realize that technology was not just complicated machines; it was also a way to turn creative ideas into something real.

That visit stayed with me. I went home wanting to learn more about programming and how the things I had seen were made. My dad's enthusiasm inspired me to consider becoming a developer one day.

2. Discovering coding at school

At secondary school, I became interested in the computer lab and the different programs available there. I wanted to understand how code worked and how people could use it to create their own programs. I explored languages and tools such as Python, Scratch, and Pascal, and enjoyed seeing how each one let me approach programming in a different way.

Later, preparing for my entrance exams took up much of my time, so I had to pause my programming practice. Even though I could not spend as much time coding, I still remembered how much I enjoyed experimenting and hoped to return to it.

3. Inspired by developers and designers

While researching for a high-school project presentation, I learned about developers and designers around the world and the ideas they bring to life through technology. I was fascinated by how they combine creativity, problem-solving, and technical skills to build useful and engaging things.

That research inspired me to learn more about technology and consider how I might create something of my own one day. It also made me curious about both sides of the process: how developers build a project and how designers make it clear and enjoyable for people to use.
